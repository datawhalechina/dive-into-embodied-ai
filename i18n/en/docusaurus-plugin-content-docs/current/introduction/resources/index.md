# Resources

A collection of papers, datasets, open source projects, and tools for learning and research in embodied AI. Each entry includes a short description, its original source, and related learning material, so you can follow a question further and put it into practice.

## Browse by resource type \{#by-type}

| Category | What you can find | Start here |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Papers and research](./papers.md) | Research questions, original papers, project pages, and our explanations | ACT, Diffusion Policy, OpenVLA |
| [Embodied AI datasets](./datasets.md) | Robot demonstrations, task types, and evaluation benchmarks | Open X-Embodiment, LIBERO |
| [Open source projects](./open-source.md) | Model code, training frameworks, and reproduction entry points | LeRobot, OpenVLA, ACT |
| [Simulation and tools](./tools.md) | Simulation engines, learning environments, and official documentation | MuJoCo, Isaac Lab, Gymnasium |

## Connect resources to learning \{#connect-to-learning}

- For an overview of the field: read the chapters of the [introduction](../intro.md).
- To understand a method: follow a paper entry to the related explanation in the [foundations](/docs/foundations/intro).
- To learn systematically: choose a [tutorial](/docs/tutorials/intro) and follow its chapters.
- To test results: find a matching experiment in the [projects](/docs/practices/intro).

The collection starts with resources related to content on this site and will grow by topic. Each entry lists its original source and the date it was compiled, so it can be traced and updated.
